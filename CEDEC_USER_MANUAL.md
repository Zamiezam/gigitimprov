## 🏠 What Is GigIT?

GigIT is a **hyperlocal student worker platform** built specifically for the **Job on Campus** ecosystem. It connects **verified UMS students** with **trusted local SME employers** — all facilitated through **CEDEC** (Centre for Entrepreneurship Development and Employability Centre).

### How It Works (The Big Picture)

```
University (UMS)
       ↓
   CEDEC Career Centre  ←  You are here (Employer)
       ↓
  Job on Campus (GigIT Platform)
       ↓
Verified Students ←→ Local SMEs
```

## 🚀 Getting Started (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Sign In

1. Go to **[gigitimprov.vercel.app](https://gigitimprov.vercel.app)**
2. Click the **"Get Started"** or **"Sign In"** button on the landing page
3. Sign in with your **Google account** or **email**
4. Select **"I'm an Employer"** when prompted for your role

> [!TIP]
> If you've never logged in before, the system will create your account automatically.

### Step 2: You're In!

Once logged in, you'll land on the **CEDEC Employer Hub** — your central dashboard for managing everything.

| Section | What It Does |

## 📝 How to Post a Campus Opportunity

This is the **most important action** — posting a job that UMS students can apply for.

### Quick Steps:

1. Click the **"Post New Opportunity"** button (blue button on the top-right of Dashboard)
2. A form will appear with the following fields:

| Field | What To Enter | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Title** | Job name | "Weekend Barista" |
| **Rate (RM)** | Hourly pay in Ringgit | "12" |
| **Duration** | Shift length | "6 Hours" |
| **Category** | Type of work | F&B / Event / Logistics / Cleaning |
| **Location** | Where the job is | "KK Town" |
| **Description** | Details about the job | "Looking for a friendly barista..." |
| **Tags** | Keywords (comma-separated) | "Customer Service, Student Friendly" |

3. Click **"Post Gig"** to publish

> [!TIP]
> **Save time!** Click **"Load Template"** at the top of the form to auto-fill from pre-made templates like Cafe Barista, Event Crew, or Warehouse Assistant. You can also click **"🎲 Random"** to generate test data quickly.

### What Happens After Posting?

- Your opportunity instantly appears on the **student browse page**
- Students near UMS campus will see it on their map and job feed
- They can **Apply** or **Book Instantly** (if you enable instant booking)

## 👥 Reviewing Student Applicants

When students apply to your posted opportunities, they appear on your **Dashboard**.

### What You See for Each Applicant:

- **Name & Photo** — The student's identity
- **⭐ Rating** — Their reliability score (out of 5.0)
- **Badge** — "Verified Student" or "High-Tier Pro"
- **No-Show Rate** — How often they've missed shifts (lower = better)
- **Distance** — How far they are from your location
- **Bio** — A brief self-introduction

### Actions You Can Take:

| Action | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Hire Now** | Instantly hire this student. They get notified. |
| **Message** | Open a real-time chat with the student |
| **AI Rank Candidates** | Let our AI score and rank all applicants by reliability |
| Click the **card** | View the student's full profile |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> When you click **"Hire Now"**, the student is immediately moved to your **Escrow & Wages** tab. The system calculates their total wage (rate × hours) and holds it in escrow.

## 💰 Escrow & Wages (Hired Workers Tab)

After hiring a student, they appear in your **Escrow & Wages** section.

### The Shift Lifecycle:

```
You Hire Student  →  Student Clocks In  →  Student Clocks Out  →  Wages Released
   (Escrow)          (Timer Starts)        (Shift Complete)       (Payment)
```

### What You'll See:

- **Worker name & avatar**
- **Gig title** they were hired for
- **Shift amount** (RM)
- **Status:** Active / Completed / Verified
- **Clock-in time** (live, updates in real-time!)
- **Payment status:** Pending / Paid

### Actions:

| Action | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Verify Shift** | Confirm the student completed the work satisfactorily |
| **Release Payment** | Release the escrowed wages to the student's wallet |
| **Send Bonus** | Reward exceptional workers with a bonus tip |

> [!NOTE]
> The system uses **real-time sync**. When a student clocks in on their phone, you'll see "Clocked In" appear on your dashboard instantly — no refresh needed!

## 💬 Messaging Students

You can chat with any applicant or hired worker in real-time.

1. Click **"Message"** on any applicant card
2. A chat panel slides in from the right
3. Type your message and press **Send**
4. Messages are delivered instantly via real-time sync

## 📊 My Opportunities Tab

This tab shows **all jobs you've ever posted**. You can:

- **Search** through your opportunities
- **Filter** by status (Open / Closed)
- **View applicant count** for each opportunity
- **Delete** opportunities you no longer need
- **Request Emergency Backup** workers if someone no-shows

## 🌱 ESG Impact Tab

Track your **Corporate Social Responsibility** contribution:

- **Students Hired** — Total students you've employed
- **Total Wages Paid** — Sum of all payments through the platform
- **First-Time Workers** — Students who got their first job through you
- **Average SWEAT™ Score** — Overall reliability of your hired workforce
- **University Partnerships** — Your connections with UMS and other institutions

> [!TIP]
> These metrics can be exported for your annual **ESG and sustainability reports**. Employers who hire through GigIT may qualify for **TalentCorp grants** and **SME tax deductions**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### "Do I need to install anything?"
**No.** GigIT is a web app. Just open the URL in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) on desktop or mobile.

### "Is it free?"
**Yes.** There are no fees for posting opportunities or hiring students during this pilot phase.

### "How do I know the students are real UMS students?"
Students are verified through their university email and student ID. Look for the **"Verified Student"** badge on their profile.

### "What if a student doesn't show up?"
Every student has a visible **No-Show Rate**. Students with 0% no-show rates are the most reliable. You can also use the **Emergency Backup Pool** feature to quickly find a replacement.

### "Can I use this on my phone?"
**Yes!** The app is fully responsive. On mobile, use the **bottom navigation bar** to switch between Dashboard, Opportunities, Escrow, Wallet, and Settings.

### "Who do I contact for support?"
Reach out to the **CEDEC Career Centre** at UMS, or contact the GigIT team directly.
